# Figure 3 saved-state reproduction

The reference PDF is Figure3.pdf, 8,281,443 bytes, SHA256 02680630188558dfbf7b8b60eda9271e02a6a027c92b711fe387945a801091b3. It shows Old 3 and Young 1. All five saved-stage panel fields are regenerated from the supplied laminin channels, raw segmentation, post-editing reference, reject/destination arrays, filter labels/statuses, final labels and the restored measurement table. The code implements display only; it does not segment, train RF or reclassify fibers.

The final publication page was assembled in Affinity Designer. Whole-field contours were visually compared and registered to its embedded raster panels. The regenerated display fields match, with small raster/resampling differences. Exact final PDF bytes and exact raster pixels are not claimed. The illustration zoom windows in display_boxes.json were recovered approximately from the published raster windows by template registration; they are presentation coordinates, not validation annotation ROI identifiers or a blinded ROI-to-section key. Panel A and later panels can have slightly different crop positions after editorial assembly. Consumers can adjust only the presentation windows without changing masks, measurements or numerical results.

Expected canonical RGB hashes cover the 10 complete saved-stage display fields before zoom boxes and scale bars. Preview PNGs show 500 µm bars on whole fields and 100 µm bars on zooms, computed at 0.36 µm/px. Layout and resampling may differ from the Affinity page. Endpoint states and selected stage masks preserve final decisions, not an individual click history.

Saved type labels and per-image calibration parameters for all six sections are included under data/calibration. These are prepared analysis references, not trained RF models and not a persistent calibration profile that the application originally restored from its checkpoint. The two labels on objects absent from the final table are preserved as saved labels but were not applied to final-table rows.
